The Discovery of Oppa

When I first saw Oppa he wasn't even 20 inches tall yet, I found him and his three siblings in a abandoned van on the side of my house. I waited three full days to see if their mom would come back, she never did, so we took them in.

Baby Kitty level 01 (Noob)

When oppa was a kitten he was sweet nothing like grown up Oppa. He would play with his toys and purr contentedly. Oppa even had mini kitten clothes and mittens.

Crazy Stories of The Oppa!

As Oppa grew older he became more rebellious and unpredictable. One time while I was cleaning my bathtub my cat decided he was going to jump onto the bar that holds up the shower curtain, causing it to fall on my head.

Oppa's Talents

Oppa is a cat of many talents, as he can use the toilet and flush it, with a bit of help on the flushing part. Oppa can also open doors though he has to jump up and open it with his paws. Oppa also has a thing for dog food, and any food in general (he will eat anything).
