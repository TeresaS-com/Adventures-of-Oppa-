🐈‍⬛ The Discovery of Oppa 🐈‍⬛

How I Met Oppa

When I first saw Oppa, he wasn't even 20 inches tall yet. I found him and his two siblings living in an abandoned van beside my house. They were all tiny and scared, and I wasn't sure where their mother was or if she was coming back. I didn't want to take them away from her if she was still caring for them, so I waited three full days, checking on them whenever I could and watching to see if their mom would return. Unfortunately, she never did. After realizing they were completely on their own, we decided to take them in and care for them. I had no idea that one of those tiny kittens, Oppa, would become such a handful and bring so many crazy adventures into my life.

Baby Kitty Level 01 (Noob)

When Oppa was a kitten, he was the sweetest little thing, nothing like the grown-up Oppa we know today. He loved playing with his toys and purring contentedly. Oppa was also very fashionable, with tiny kitten clothes my mother and I made by hand. Back then, we thought we had rescued a sweet, innocent kitten. Oh, how wrong we were! Turns out, kitten Oppa was just the free trial. The full version came with a lot more attitude!

Crazy Stories of Oppa!

🐈‍⬛ As Oppa grew older, he became more rebellious and unpredictable. One time, while I was cleaning my bathtub, my cat decided he was going to jump onto the bar that holds up the shower curtain, causing it to fall on my head. 🐈‍⬛

Oppa's Talents

Oppa is a cat of many talents. He can use the toilet and flush it, although he still needs some work on the flushing part.

Oppa can also open doors, though he has to jump up and push them with his paws.

Oppa has a thing for dog food and food in general. He will eat almost anything!

Oppa can beg and sit. Sometimes, if he's feeling particularly mischievous, he'll attack your ankles.
