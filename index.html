🐈‍⬛The Discovery of Oppa🐈‍⬛

When I first saw Oppa, he wasn't even 20 inches tall yet. I found him and his two other siblings living in an abandoned van on the side of my house. They were all to tiny and scared, I wasn't sure where their mother was or if she was even coming back. I didn't want to take them away from her if she was still caring for them, so I waited three full days, checking on them every second I could and watching to see if their mom would return . Unfortunetly, she never did. After realizing they were completely on their own, we decided to take them in and care for them. I had no idea at the time that one of those tiny kittens, Oppa, would endup becoming such a handful and bring to many crazy adventures into my life.

Baby Kitty level 01 (Noob)

When Oppa was a kitten , he was the sweeteest little thing-nothing like grown-up Oppa we know today. He loved playing with his toys, purring contentedly. Oppa was also very fashionable, with tiny kitten clothes me and my mother made from hand. Back then we thought we had recued a sweet, innocent kitten, oh how we were wrong. Turns out, kitten Oppa was just the free trial- the full version came witha lot more attitude!

Crazy Stories of The Oppa!

Oppa's Talents

Oppa also has a thing for dog food, and any food in general (he will eat anything).

Oppa can also beg and sit and sometimes if he's feeling particularly mischievous, he'll attack your ankles.
